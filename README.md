# The World Today — Sunday, 22 November 2026

One image summing up the world news of Sunday, 22 November 2026: 31 stories
across 7 regions — COP31 collapsing in Antalya, tankers hit near the Strait
of Hormuz, a drone strike on a Russian warship at Benghazi, the Democrats'
largest House majority since 1992, and more. Every story is dated 22 November
2026 and sourced from AP, Reuters, CNN, Le Monde, KIRO, TBS, KRON, KPFA,
ASEAN Now, WebIndia123 and Standard Topic.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png`. The image is 1024×1024, built with Python PIL and DejaVu
fonts: serif headlines, one-line summaries, outlet names only as sources.
